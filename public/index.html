Auto Share

Share your Facebook posts to all your groups in one click.

1 · Facebook login

Only needed the first time. A Chrome window opens, so log in there and leave it open.

2 · What to share

Open your post on Facebook → click its time stamp (or ⋯ → Copy link) → paste the link here.

3 · Groups

One link per line.
    Waits 2–4s between groups so Facebook doesn't flag you.

    Leave groups

    Load the groups you're in, delete the lines for any you want to keep, then press Leave. Leaving can't be undone — you'd have to ask to join again.
